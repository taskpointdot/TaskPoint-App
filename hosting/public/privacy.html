Privacy Policy for Taskpoint

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Taskpoint ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile app that connects people who need everyday tasks done ("seekers") with people willing to do them ("providers"). This policy explains what information Taskpoint collects, why, and how it is used, stored, and protected.

By creating a Taskpoint account or using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryWhat we collectWhy
Account & phone verification Phone number, one-time password (OTP) verification status To create your account and verify you're a real person, using Firebase Authentication
Identity verification CNIC (national identity card) front and back photos, captured via camera or uploaded from your gallery To verify the identity of providers and seekers and reduce fraud on the platform
Profile information Name, profile photo, role (seeker or provider), ratings and reviews To display your profile to other users and build trust on the platform
Location data Real-time device location while a job is in progress (used to track worker/customer location for a job) To show live job tracking between the provider and seeker during an active task
Job content Job descriptions, voice recordings (for voice-based job posting), photos taken to document completed jobs To post, fulfill, and verify completion of tasks
Chat messages Messages exchanged between seekers and providers, and messages sent to the in-app AI assistant To let users coordinate on jobs and get help from the AI assistant
Payments & wallet Digital wallet balance, top-up amounts, transaction history, earnings records, payment proof screenshots (for top-ups) To process payments between users and maintain accurate transaction records
Emergency & safety data Emergency contacts you add, and SOS alerts you trigger (including your location at the time of the alert) To notify your emergency contacts if you use the in-app SOS feature
Device & usage data Device identifiers, linked/logged-in devices, app diagnostics, push notification tokens To secure your account, manage logged-in devices, and deliver notifications

2. Permissions the App Requests

  • Camera — to capture CNIC photos and job-completion photos
  • Photo library / storage — to upload CNIC photos or a profile picture from your gallery
  • Microphone — to record voice job postings
  • Location — to track an in-progress job and to attach location to SOS alerts
  • Notifications — to alert you about job offers, messages, and account activity

You can deny or later revoke any of these permissions in your device settings; some features (e.g. CNIC verification, live job tracking) won't work without the relevant permission.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account (phone/OTP authentication)
  • To verify your identity as a seeker or provider (CNIC review)
  • To match seekers with providers and enable job posting, negotiation, and completion
  • To process wallet top-ups, track earnings, and maintain transaction history
  • To provide live location tracking during an active job
  • To send emergency SOS alerts to your chosen emergency contacts
  • To power the in-app AI assistant when you chat with it
  • To send push notifications about job activity, messages, and account updates
  • To investigate reported problems, fraud, or abuse on the platform

4. How Your Information Is Stored

Taskpoint uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage) to store account data, job/chat records, and uploaded files such as CNIC photos, job-completion photos, and payment proofs. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and protected by Firebase's access-control rules, which restrict data access to your own account and, where relevant, the other party in a job (e.g. your matched provider/seeker).

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • Between matched users — a seeker and provider matched on a job can see each other's name, profile photo, rating, and job-relevant chat/location, so they can coordinate the task
  • With service providers — such as Google Firebase, which hosts and processes app data on our behalf, under its own data-processing terms
  • For legal reasons — if required by law, or to investigate fraud, safety issues, or violations of our terms
  • With your emergency contacts — only when you trigger an SOS alert

6. Data Retention

We retain account, job, and transaction data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to resolve disputes, maintain financial records, and meet legal obligations. CNIC images are retained only for identity-verification and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Your Choices

  • You can edit your profile information from within the app
  • You can manage or remove emergency contacts from the Emergency Contacts screen
  • You can manage devices linked to your account from the Manage Devices screen
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below

8. Children's Privacy

Taskpoint is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
